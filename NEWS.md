# The stories on the front page — Friday 20 November 2026

Every story below happened or was first reported on 20 November 2026.
The 19 November stories that dominated the previous day (the Shenzhen
trilateral summit, the ICJ genocide ruling, the Anthropic IPO debut) are
deliberately left out: this page is about 20 November alone.

## 1. Lead — Kyiv's bridges hit in an overnight missile barrage

Russia attacked Kyiv with ballistic missiles overnight into 20 November,
triggering explosions and fires across several districts and damaging three
bridges over the Dnipro (Northern, Podilskyi and Southern/Darnytskyi).
Mayor Vitali Klitschko reported the damage on Telegram; no casualties had
been reported yet. The attack followed Putin's warning of retaliation for
Ukraine's strikes on the Crimean Bridge.

Sources: Kyiv City Military Administration (1:22 AM UTC, 20 Nov); Kyiv
Independent; Mayor Vitali Klitschko via Telegram; Reuters.

## 2. The Gulf — Houthis claim four Saudi targets; tanker hits mine

The Houthis claimed strikes on four targets in Saudi Arabia, and Saudi air
defences shot down Houthi drones. Separately, a tanker caught fire after
hitting a mine east of the Strait of Hormuz.

Sources: APA (Houthi claim, 20 Nov); Saudi-led coalition statement;
Daily Baku live feed (20 Nov).

## 3. Britain · Europe — UK and EU agree youth mobility scheme

Britain and the EU agreed a youth mobility scheme, letting young people
live and work on either side.

Source: BBC, "UK and EU to agree youth mobility scheme, BBC understands",
20 November 2026.

## 4. United States — US sanctions bikie gang and 'Ndrangheta

The US sanctioned Australia's Comancheros motorcycle gang and Italy's
'Ndrangheta mafia network, blocking assets under US jurisdiction, with a
limited wind-down licence for companies in the Vescio network.

Source: US Treasury action as reported 20 November 2026.

## 5. Japan — 13,000 march in Tokyo against Takaichi

## 6. Technology — California probes Nvidia over China chips
